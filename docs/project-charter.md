# Project Charter: CMMS for Construction Equipment Assets

## 1. Background
Abdul Mohsen Al-Tamimi Group requires a centralized Computerized Maintenance Management System (CMMS) to track, schedule, and optimize maintenance of its construction fleet and equipment. Existing spreadsheets and manual processes lead to data silos, delays, and under-utilized assets.

## 2. Purpose
Implement a scalable, IoT-enabled CMMS to:
- Standardize asset registration and documentation  
- Automate preventive and corrective maintenance workflows  
- Provide real-time asset utilization and health dashboards  

## 3. Objectives
1. **Deploy Core CMMS Functionality** by Q3 2025: Asset registry, work-order engine, inventory module.  
2. **Integrate IoT Feeds** for equipment hours, fuel, and GPS by Q4 2025.  
3. **Achieve 85% Fleet Utilization** and 20% Downtime Reduction within 6 months post-go-live.  

## 4. Scope
**In Scope**  
- All heavy equipment (cranes, excavators, loaders) and fleet vehicles.  
- Preventive, predictive, and reactive maintenance modules.  
- Spare-parts inventory tracking and reorder alerts.

**Out of Scope**  
- Human resources management.  
- Financial accounting modules beyond cost-tracking.
